Help

How the scheduler works
• You may hold one laundry reservation per week.
• When claiming, auto-assign is recommended, especially at busy times. You may optionally choose a currently available working machine, but it is only guaranteed once your claim is successfully saved. Out-of-order machines cannot be selected. The machine status list above Available slots shows current operational status.
• If somebody takes your preferred machine first or it becomes out of order, your reservation is not created; refresh and try another machine or use auto-assign.
• You can move or cancel your reservation only until one hour after the slot starts.
• You can change machines throughout your running slot, for example if your current machine stops working, using auto-assign or choosing another free working machine. If the change fails, your original reservation stays unchanged. After the first hour, the slot is committed: moving and cancelling are locked, but machine changes remain possible until the slot ends.
• You can report a machine problem at any time, even if you are not currently using that machine.
• If a machine already has reports, the app will show that, but you can still submit your own description.
• A scheduler report does not disable the machine automatically. The dorm administrator reviews reports and decides whether to mark a machine out of order.
Important: reporting here is only for the laundry scheduler.
It does not contact maintenance. The machine will not get repaired unless the fault is also reported in MojŠtudent.
Report the fault in MojŠtudent →
• Use Public schedule at the top to view existing reservations. After the next cycle opens, refresh the slots to see the new schedule.
• If you forget your access code, use your recovery key to reset it.
If something goes wrong
• If a slot appears available but fails to claim, refresh the slots and try again.
• If the scheduler is busy and cannot complete your action, refresh the slots and try again.
• If your reservation becomes committed, the move/cancel decision window has passed. You can still change to another free working machine until the end of your slot.
• If sign-in fails, double-check your room number and access code.
• If you keep having issues, contact the dorm representative.
